## Open-Source GitHub Projects



- **[Distilled CS](https://github.com/saneeshnp/distilled-cs)**  

  Open framework for assessing Customer Success team maturity, health scoring approaches, journey design, and rebuilding CS strategy in a vendor-neutral way.



- **[Customer Success health scoring tools](https://github.com/search?q=customer+health+score+OR+churn+prediction+OR+expansion+scoring)**  

  Python CLI tools and scripts that calculate multi-dimensional health scores, churn risk tiers, and expansion opportunity rankings from usage, engagement, and support data.



- **[Customer Success MCP / AI agent projects](https://github.com/search?q=customer+success+MCP+OR+CS+agent)**  

  Production-oriented open-source agents and MCP servers for health monitoring, onboarding automation, churn prediction, and retention workflows.



- **[n8n + CRM automation stacks](https://github.com/n8n-io/n8n)**  

  Open-source workflow automation frequently used to build custom CS playbooks, health score updates, risk alerts, and expansion triggers across CRM, product, and support tools.



- **[Twenty / Odoo / SuiteCRM extensions](https://github.com/twentyhq/twenty)**  

  Open-source CRM platforms that can be extended with custom health score fields, renewal tracking, and expansion opportunity management.



- **[Zapier-style health agents & retention workflows](https://github.com/search?q=customer+health+agent+OR+churn+prevention+workflow)**  

  Community projects that monitor multi-source customer signals and trigger automated retention or expansion actions.



- **[Analytics & segmentation helpers](https://github.com/search?q=customer+segmentation+OR+health+score+dashboard)**  

  Open-source dashboards and notebooks for visualizing customer health, cohort retention, and expansion potential.



- **[Other CS frameworks & maturity models](https://github.com/search?q=customer+success+framework+OR+CS+maturity)**  

  Emerging open resources for structuring CS operations, playbooks, and metrics.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options



- **Product analytics foundations**: PostHog, Matomo, or similar tools that feed usage data into health models.

- **Support & ticket signals**: Integrations with open-source helpdesks (Chatwoot, etc.) for support-volume health factors.

- **Data pipelines**: Airbyte + dbt patterns to unify CRM, product, billing, and support data for scoring.

- **BI layers**: Metabase or Superset for CS dashboards and expansion opportunity reporting.

- **Playbook automation**: n8n, Temporal, or custom scripts for onboarding journeys and risk interventions.

- Spreadsheet + script hybrids still common for early-stage health scoring and expansion tracking.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**:  

Start with an open-source CRM (**Twenty**, **Odoo**, or **SuiteCRM**) and layer custom health scoring logic, **n8n** workflows for playbooks, and open analytics tools for visibility.  

Use frameworks like **Distilled CS** for process maturity and open health-scoring scripts for core metrics.  

Commercial platforms (Gainsight, Vitally, Planhat, ChurnZero, Totango, etc.) provide polished health models, journey orchestration, revenue intelligence, and CSM productivity features that require substantial engineering effort to replicate.  

Many teams begin with open-source building blocks and graduate to commercial CS platforms as account volume and complexity grow.
